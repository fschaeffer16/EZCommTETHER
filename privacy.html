EZvoxa Privacy Policy

EZ Voice LLC · Effective August 27, 2026
The short version: EZvoxa is a communication app for people who cannot speak. Your boards, photos, and personalization stay on your device. We have no accounts, no ads, no analytics trackers, and we never sell data. The only information that ever leaves your device is what a feature needs at the moment you use it — and this page lists every case.

What stays on your device

  • Board setup and personalization — every button, phrase, and layout choice.
  • Photos you add to personalize buttons. They are stored only on the device and are never uploaded.

What leaves your device, and when

  • Spoken phrases (AI voice). When the AI voice is on, the text of a tapped phrase is sent to our server, which uses a speech provider to generate the audio. Phrases are cached so repeats are not re-sent. Phrase text is not linked to any identity.
  • Emergency SOS. When you (and only when you) trigger SOS, your message and your live location are sent to the family contacts you configured. Location is used at that moment only — EZvoxa does not track location in the background, ever.
  • Messages to family. When you send a text through EZvoxa, the message and the chosen contact are processed by our server to deliver it.
  • Voice notes. Short recordings left for family members pass through our server for delivery and are not used for anything else.
  • Family Sync. If you turn on sync, your settings are stored in our database under a random code so your family's devices can share them. The code is the only identifier — there is no account, name, or email attached.

What we never do

  • No advertising, and no third-party analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of data with anyone, for any reason.
  • No accounts and no profiles — we could not identify you if we wanted to.

Children

EZvoxa is set up and managed by a parent or caregiver. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no accounts, no ads, no social features, and no communication with anyone except the family contacts a parent configures.

Emergencies

EZvoxa's SOS alerts your own family contacts. It is not a substitute for calling 911 or your local emergency services.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. If you used Family Sync and want the synced copy of your settings removed from our database, email us with your sync code and we will delete it.
