Talker: buyers that year times the one-time price. Family Tether: the average of the year's starting and ending subscribers, times the monthly price, times twelve. Schools: district seats at the volume price when 20 or more seats, else the list price. Store fee applies to everything sold through a store. RevenueCat is 1% of store sales once tracked revenue passes $2,500 a month. Cloud and fixed: texts and alerts at Twilio's rates, the voice per family, the Twilio number, Vercel, Codemagic, Apple's yearly fee, Google's and 10DLC one-time fees. Refunds and acquisition are the model's carried-over assumptions.
