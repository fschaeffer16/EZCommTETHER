## Headline answers (detail and sources in the files)

- **"Nobody else does it" is false as stated** — WordPower ships hand-authored "Intelligent
  Jumps" and "logical next words." **True in the sharp form:** nobody does *adaptive*
  next-word navigation learned from the individual child, and no symbol AAC uses AI routing.
- **No product plays a sign clip on button press as built-in behavior** — Frank's
  one-button/three-cues idea would be first of its kind.
- **Sync benchmark:** CoughDrop (cloud-native, free supervisor accounts) + Grid 3's invited
  remote editors. The Family-Code structure beats both on data ownership if propagation is
  real-time.
- **Growth is the industry's broken axis** — every incumbent sacrifices customization,
  motor-plan stability, or migration-free growth; holding all three at once is the standard
  to set.
