# AAC products used in US elementary schools: product history and update dating

Prepared for Frank Schaeffer, EZ Voice LLC. Slice: product history and update dating.

All facts below were read from the maker's own site, Apple's App Store (apps.apple.com and Apple's lookup API at itunes.apple.com), Google Play, or the Wayback Machine copy of the maker's own page. Date read for everything: 10 September 2026 (the date given for this task; the session clock showed 9 Sep 2026 UTC). Anything not read is marked "not checked" or "blocked". No forums, blogs, resellers, or news sites were used.

How App Store dates were obtained: each apps.apple.com listing embeds a "Version History" block (the 25 most recent versions with release notes and dates). "First release" comes from Apple's lookup API field `releaseDate` (https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=...), which is the date the app first went live on the App Store. Prices are the `price=` value in the listing's own buy button (`buyParams` with the app's `salableAdamId`) and the lookup API `formattedPrice`; the two agreed for every app except where noted.
