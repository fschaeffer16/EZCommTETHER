Talker: buyers that year times the one-time price. Family Tether: the average of the year's starting and ending subscribers, times the monthly price, times twelve. Schools and clinics: institutional seats at the volume price when 20 or more seats, else the list price; clinical seats are therapists (Frank, 8 Sep) and are priced at the school rate, as the page prices them. Store fee applies to everything sold through a store. RevenueCat is 1% of store sales once tracked revenue passes $2,500 a month. Cloud and fixed: texts and alerts at Twilio's rates, the voice per family, the Twilio number, Vercel, Codemagic, Apple's yearly fee, Google's and 10DLC one-time fees. Refunds and acquisition are the model's carried-over assumptions. To change an input, use the finance widget; the same defaults feed both pages.

Where operations stand

What the server does with each RevenueCat event

This is what api/plan.js does today, not a sketch. The endpoint is /api/plan on the app's domain: ez-comm-tether.vercel.app on the family lane, app.ezvoxa.com on the customer lane. Every event must carry the shared secret in its Authorization header or it is refused. The same event id is never applied twice. A hardship grant made by us is never overridden by a store event.

Where the pasted hub's sketch differs from the code

  • SOS is never locked. The sketch's EXPIRATION comment says it "locks remote sync and SOS texting". SOS Family Alert is free, final (Frank, 2 Sep 2026), and api/sos.js never reads the plan. An expiry locks everyday texting and the natural voice. The talker keeps talking.
  • A lifetime Core Talker is not something our server grants. The sketch grants it on NON_RENEWING_PURCHASE. The one-time app purchase belongs to the store and the buyer's store account; our server only ever tracks the family's Tether plan. Today NON_RENEWING_PURCHASE turns the family plan on, which is right only while the subscription is the sole product. If the Core Talker is ever sold as a one-time in-app purchase inside a free app, that line has to change, and it must not turn the Tether on. Frank's call on how the Talker is sold decides it; not built either way.
  • Refunds. The sketch refunds on CANCELLATION when cancel_reason is CUSTOMER_SUPPORT. Today CANCELLATION keeps the plan to the expiry the store gave, and no refund branch exists, because RevenueCat's event names and that field could not be read from revenuecat.com (blocked from here). The sales page counts refunds under Apple's names only. blocked Way in: on revenuecat.com search "webhook event types and fields", then paste the event list and the CANCELLATION fields.
  • Endpoint. The sketch hard-codes app.ezvoxa.com. Until a release is cut, the live endpoint is the family lane's, and that is the URL to give RevenueCat first.
