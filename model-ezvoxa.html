How the market is built (every step sourced): population 12+ → 0.5% require AAC (ASHA/UK prevalence) → ×65% reachable by a touch app (AAC-on-iOS survey: 90% of users use an iPad, 25%+ an iPhone, only 15–20% a dedicated device) → your penetration. Penetration is anchored so 5-year totals stay a fraction of Proloquo2Go's install base — the 15-year category leader, used by "hundreds of thousands" worldwide. Covers all causes 12+ (autism, apraxia, CP, Down syndrome, stroke, ALS, TBI). US includes English- & Spanish-speaking families; Spain + Latin America are the added Spanish markets. Latin America penetration is discounted for willingness-to-pay + lower diagnosis/awareness only — not device access, since LatAm is mobile-first (~76% smartphone adoption), which a phone product fits.
School districts (education channel): the US has 13,194 public school districts (NCES 2023–24); Florida alone has 67. Under IDEA, AAC written into a student's IEP must be provided by the district at no cost to the family — school AAC purchasing is an existing legal obligation, not a new behavior. Pricing: $99/student/yr (net ~$97, invoiced), all premium features + SLP usage log; school seats span IDEA ages 3–21 incl. 18–21 transition programs. Scale check: 0.5% prevalence ≈ ~200 AAC-need students in a 40k-student district, so 50 seats/district is ~a quarter of one district's need. Ramp is a labeled assumption, pilot-gated: Year 1 is the free pilot at the founder's son's school (the ESE director requested the trial unprompted). Floor: 10 districts, classroom-scale (30 seats). Mid: 28 districts (fewer than half of Florida alone) × 50 seats. Ceiling: the cascade — pilots trigger state networks → 200 districts (1.5% of US) × 50 seats, with a real education-support hire from Yr 3 (~$75–95k/yr) instead of the ~$2k conference budget the small cases carry. Competitive note: TD Snap classroom licenses are free — the school pitch is usage/abandonment, never price. Adult institutional programs (day programs, voc rehab, group homes) are not modeled — consumer sales only; upside on purpose. Schools also build Phase 3's SLP-evaluation network and device-trial documentation.
Dedicated device (Phase 2 — after certification): 15% of buyers take a dedicated device (survey: 15–20% of AAC users use one). Split 30% funded / 70% cash — funded kept small on purpose: Medicare funds only ~2,000–3,000 SGDs/year (CMS), a tiny clinician-gated market a new entrant enters slowly. Net/device: cash ~$94 (iPhone 11 build); funded ~$2,200 (sourced ~$4,500 reimbursement − device − warranty/infra reserve − DME-partner cut). Requires FDA listing, PDAC coding & DME enrollment first.
