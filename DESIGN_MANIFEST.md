## What went wrong with my last three documents

I was answering the question *"what in here is Evan's?"* You were asking *"what does the
designer have to draw?"* Those produce different lists, and mine kept coming up short. This
one is the second question.

Two things I got outright wrong and want on the record:

- **I marked Food "clean."** It is not. `hamburger` is Evan standing under the golden arches
  in a McDonald's. `french_fries` is the McDonald's carton. And `coke`, `ginger_ale` (Canada
  Dry), `gatorade_red / orange / blue`, `naked`, `hot_fries` (Fritos), `salt_vinegar_chips`
  (Lay's) and `ketchup` (Heinz) are all trademarked products.
- **Real phone numbers.** The template pulled Frank's and Raniyah's actual numbers from the
  server onto the Family board, and a tap on Text would have sent a real message. Both fixed
  and shipped today — but I should have caught them in the first audit.

## The shape of the job

| | Buttons |
|---|---|
| Need drawn artwork | **98** |
| Already delivered and wired | 32 |
| **Still to draw** | **66** |
| Sentence buttons — glyph, not artwork | 128 |

The 128 are things like *"Can I have a break?"* and *"I need it quiet."* Those are sentences,
not objects. A small glyph beside the words reads faster than a picture, and drawing 128
pictures of abstract statements would take months and make the boards harder to scan. My
recommendation is to leave them as glyphs and restyle the button chrome to match.

## 13. Format, every time

- **One file per icon.** Not a contact sheet — a sheet gives about 244 pixels an icon and a
  tile needs 525 on an iPhone Pro Max.
- **1024 × 1024**, square, black background, subject centred with margin.
- **No text baked in** — no labels, no headers, no numbers inside clocks or calendars.
- **No brands.** No arches, no logos, no packaging anyone would recognise.
- **Same prompt across a batch** so the set holds together.
- **Named exactly as listed** and **zipped.**
