EZvoxa

A voice for people who don't have one.

A communication app for nonverbal teenagers and adults — on the phone they already carry, at a price a family can actually pay.

Why it exists

My son Evan is sixteen and doesn't speak. His communication device was an iPad running software that cost thousands of dollars, and he stopped wanting to carry it years ago — too big, too clinical, built for a much younger child.

So I built him something else: on a phone, in a voice that sounds like a teenager instead of a robot, with the words he actually uses. It has a button that reaches his family in an emergency, and one that plays him a voice message from his mother.

He uses it every day. Other families keep asking where they can get it — so I'm making it available.

— Frank Schaeffer

What it does

  • Speaks in a voice that fits. Age-appropriate, not a robot and not a grown man.
  • Built around real life. Family, food, school, feelings, routines — and every button can be changed to use your own words and photos.
  • Emergency button. One tap reaches the people on your list, with your location.
  • Voice messages from family. They hear their own mother's voice, not a transcript.
  • Runs on a phone. Pocket-sized, so it's actually with them.
  • Customising is free. Photos, buttons and phrases cost nothing extra, ever.
