His Voice Was Always There

EZvoxa grew from one decision: stop asking Evan to meet the world on its terms, and start meeting him on his.

My son Evan was born in August 2010.

I am not a doctor or a speech pathologist.

My son Evan was born in August 2010.

I am not a doctor or a speech pathologist. I am just a father who has spent the past 16 years in silence with my son, learning what every look, every nuance of his face, his eyes, and his body language are trying to tell me.

Until finally, it dawned on me to stop being his teacher and start being his student.

And from that, we found the voice that was always inside him.
