Why now

The students changed. The tools did not.

The leading communication apps for nonverbal students were released between 2009 and 2012. The number of students they were built for has grown several times over since then.

2.9×students with autism served under IDEA, 2008-09 to 2022-23

336,000 students in 2008-09. 980,000 in 2022-23. The current apps launched in that first window and have not been rebuilt for the students who arrived after.

Source: National Center for Education Statistics, Digest of Education Statistics, table 204.30. App release years from Apple’s App Store listings: Proloquo2Go 2009, TouchChat 2010, LAMP Words for Life 2012.

7.4×Florida students aged 6 to 21 with autism, 2005 to 2024

7,918 students in 2005. 58,848 in 2024. Over the same years, Florida students with disabilities of every kind in that age band grew 12 percent. This is not a general rise. It is autism.

Source: US Department of Education, IDEA Section 618 Part B child count, state-level files, data.ed.gov.

+79%St. Lucie County school-age students with autism, 2020-21 to 2024-25

579 students in 2020-21. 1,035 in 2024-25. Autism is now 15.5 percent of the district’s school-age students with disabilities.

Source: US Department of Education, IDEA Section 618 Part B child count, LEA-level files, 2020-21 through 2024-25.

How it works

One license. Three ways to install. No district is told no.

PATH A · THE DEFAULT

App Store or Google Play, by your private link

EZvoxa Pro is an unlisted store app. It does not appear in search. Your district’s private link installs it on any iPad, iPhone or Android device, managed or not, and it updates through the store like any other app.

Each device joins your organization with a join code from your portal. The code counts one seat. No student names are entered anywhere.

PATH B · ANY DEVICE, NO STORE ACCOUNT

From the web, on any device

Install from app.ezvoxa.com to the home screen, enter the join code, done. Same app, same code, no Apple ID or Google account needed. This is the path for a classroom that has devices but no store accounts.

Districts that require everything through Apple School Manager can receive EZvoxa Pro as a custom app for their organization ID, invoiced by us.

PATH C · ON REQUEST

On devices we ship

For programs that want a dedicated communication device: supervised phones locked to the app, shipped configured with your join code. Priced separately on the quote when a district asks for it.

Updates once or twice a year. Nothing on a device changes on its own between releases, and your administrator gets a release note by email before each one. Backup and restore is built in: a device can export its buttons and settings to a file and restore them on a replacement device. The file stays with the district. We never hold it.

Security and privacy

No student data. That is a design rule, not a setting.

  • No student names, records or identifiers on our servers, ever. The app’s data stays on the device.
  • Our server holds only your organization, its seat count, its administrators, its devices by join code, and its orders.
  • Portal sign-in by emailed link. No passwords to leak. Sessions expire. Every action is logged with who and when.
  • Join codes expire and are single-use per device. Remove a device from the portal and its seat is freed and that device is locked.
  • Purchase orders, invoices, W-9 and insurance certificate are stored in your portal, so your purchasing office gets everything from one page.

EZvoxa Pro contains no family features: no texting, no family alerts, no shared contacts, no location. Those belong to the family edition and are not in the school build.
